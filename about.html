Meet The Directors

Mr. Boniface U. Ashiwhobel

Managing Director / Chief Executive Officer Global Avsec Security Limited | Ashiverb Security & Guards Limited

Boniface U. Ashiwhobel, M.Sc.; B.Sc.; Adsom.; Avsec PM.; CPP; PCI, is a distinguished security expert and accomplished leader with over two decades of experience in aviation, industrial, and corporate security. He currently serves as the Managing Director and Chief Executive Officer of both Global Avsec Security Limited and Ashiverb Security & Guards Limited. Renowned for his strategic foresight and operational acumen, Mr. Ashiwhobel possesses a deep ability to align security operations with broader business objectives across diverse sectors. He is board-certified by the American Society for Industrial Security (ASIS International), holding both the Certified Protection Professional (CPP) and Professional Certified Investigator (PCI) designations—globally recognized benchmarks in security leadership. In February 2023, Mr. Ashiwhobel relocated to Canada, where he joined GardaWorld as the Branch Manager for Regina, Saskatchewan, bringing his wealth of expertise to the North American security landscape. From March 2017 to February 2023, he served as Security Manager for West, East, and Central Africa at Kimberly-Clark Corporation, where he led multi-regional security strategy and risk mitigation efforts and received the security transformation award from Kimberly Clark. Prior to that, he was Area Security Manager for West Africa at DHL Express (2015–2017), overseeing critical logistics and personnel safety in high-risk environments. His leadership journey also includes impactful tenures with Halogen Security Company Limited, where he was Group Head for Lagos and Western Region in 2013, and later Territorial Head, Eastern Region (2014–2015). His earlier contributions to aviation security began with Aviation Logistics & Management Limited (ALML), where he managed operations for Virgin Atlantic, and subsequently held the position of Head of Security at Virgin Nigeria Airways / Air Nigeria Development Limited from 2006 to 2012. The air operator was adjudged the Best Security Conscious airline in West and Central Africa. Mr. Ashiwhobel’s academic background includes a Master’s Degree (Distinction) in Political Science and an Advanced Diploma in Security Operations and Management, both from the University of Lagos. He also holds a Bachelor of Science in Political Science from the University of Calabar. His professional development is equally impressive, having completed numerous international training programs, including:

  • • Senior Management of Aviation Security (SMAS) – IATA, Lagos
  • • Aviation Security Professional Manager (AvSec PM) – ICAO & Concordia University
  • • UK National Aviation Security Manager Course Level 3 – Department of Transport
  • • Advanced Forgery Detection – UK Immigration, Heathrow & Gatwick Airport
  • • Security Management Systems (SEMS – Distinction) – IATA Geneva, Switzerland
  • • Airline Security Operations – IATA Miami, Florida, USA
  • • Security Manager Certification – International Security & Defense System, LLC (USA)
Earlier in his career, he co-founded Uzbon Security Solutions Limited (2003–2005) and began his security profession at Corporate Guards in 1997, rising to become Head of Client Services. He also served as a Supernumerary Police Officer with Mobil Producing Nigeria Unlimited from 1987 to 1990. Mr. Ashiwhobel is widely respected for his proactive leadership style, dependable service, and result-oriented approach. He continues to shape the security industry through innovation, mentorship, and unwavering dedication to excellence.
